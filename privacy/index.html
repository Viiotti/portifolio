Privacy

What this site collects

Short version: almost nothing, and nothing that follows you around.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Fonts and every other file are served from this site, so your browser makes no requests to third parties while you read it.

The numbers in the footer (load time, page weight, cookie count) are measured in your browser, shown to you, and sent nowhere.

Stored on your device

If you switch between light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the key theme. It never leaves your device. Clearing site data removes it.

Contact form

The contact form does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email app with the message written, and you decide whether to send it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service running; see the GitHub privacy statement. I do not receive those logs.

Questions

Email rafaelviotti@gmail.com. If anything on this page changes, it will say so here first. Back home.
