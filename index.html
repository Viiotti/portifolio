The short version

Operations taught me what production actually costs

I spent two years on the operations side of critical systems: watching dashboards at 3am, triaging incidents from L1 through L3, holding SLAs, and running the backup and recovery routines nobody notices until they fail.

That work gave me a specific instinct — ask what breaks, what it costs per request, and how we would know if it degraded. When I started automating those routines in Python, the same instinct carried over. It is also why I care about measuring an LLM pipeline rather than shipping one that only demos well.

I am now deliberately moving from infrastructure operations into AI automation: Python services, agent workflows, retrieval systems, and the evaluation layer around them. Studying Systems Analysis and Development at PUC Minas alongside.
