The short version

Operations first. Models second.

I started in hardware, repairing machines while finishing a technical degree. From there: field support, then monitoring a transport company's network, then analysing operational graphs and integrity signals at a gaming operator. Years of asking what broke, why, and how we would have known sooner.

Then I spent six months as the sole architect and developer of a company's AI integrations — a multi-agent knowledge system with retrieval, autonomous workflows, and data-integrity controls built in rather than bolted on.

The operations background is the whole difference. Most people arrive at RAG from the model side and discover reliability later. I arrived from the side where things page you at 3am, so I build measurement and recovery first — and treat the vector index as derived, rebuildable state rather than something precious.

What I can do, and where I did it

No percentage bars. Each line names where the experience comes from, so you can ask me about it directly.

Retrieval & Vector Infrastructure PRIMARY

Qdrant · collection-per-domain isolation · chunking strategy · index rebuild and recovery · scroll profiling

Operate a self-hosted deployment of 20 collections and 16,005 points. Recovered a corrupted collection by treating the index as derived state and rebuilding from source — which is why I argue reindexing is a feature, not a failure.

Embeddings & Local Inference PRIMARY

Ollama · nomic-embed-text (768d) · local model serving · batch embedding

Chose local embeddings over a hosted API: zero cost per query, no rate limits, and embeddings that never leave the machine. The trade-off is RAM and CPU per batch, which is the right trade for this workload.

Agent Orchestration STRONG

LangGraph · bounded delegation · isolated child contexts · fail-closed design

Built orchestration with hard limits — depth-one spawning, capped concurrency, leaf agents that cannot delegate or write memory — and an inline fallback when a delegated provider is unavailable.

LLM Observability STRONG

LangFuse v3 self-hosted · ClickHouse · Postgres · MinIO · Redis · trace instrumentation

Run the full self-hosted stack rather than a SaaS trace endpoint, and instrumented a retrieval pilot end to end to validate ingestion.

Infrastructure & Operations PRIMARY

Linux · nginx · WireGuard-restricted access · systemd services · backup and recovery drills

Years of network and infrastructure monitoring before AI, now applied to my own stack: VPN-only boundary, and rehearsed recovery for application state, vector collections and knowledge files.

Retrieval Evaluation BUILDING

golden datasets · recall@k · latency percentiles · cost per query · embedding model comparison

My current focus, and stated plainly: I have the infrastructure and I am building the measurement layer on top of it. Most RAG systems never get one — which is exactly why it is worth doing properly.
